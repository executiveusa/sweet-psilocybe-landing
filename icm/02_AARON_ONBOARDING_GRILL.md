> - Ask **ONE question at a time**.

## The 6 Onboarding Questions (One at a Time)

### Question 1 — Sale & Order Alert Email
- **Ask:** "Where should I send your instant alerts whenever someone buys merch from your store or reaches out about a partnership?"
- **Recommendation:** "I recommend a dedicated brand email like `orders@sweetpsilocybe.com` (or your favorite personal email that forwards there) so your personal privacy stays protected while you never miss a sale."

### Question 2 — Social Media Accounts (TikTok, Instagram, X, Facebook)
- **Ask:** "Right now the social icons at the bottom of your site point to the sign-up pages for TikTok, Instagram, X, and Facebook. Have you already created any `@sweetpsilocybe` handles, or would you like me to guide you step-by-step to claim them first?"
- **Recommendation:** "Claiming `@sweetpsilocybe` on TikTok and Instagram first gives us the fastest reach for faceless mushroom art, short science clips, and merch drops."

### Question 3 — Merch Pricing & Profit Margin
- **Ask:** "Your first 10 holiday & winter pieces (Hoodie `$54.99`, Crewneck `$44.99`, Blanket `$59.99`, Beanie `$28.99`, Mug `$18.99`, Tumbler `$32.99`, Ornament `$16.99`, Long Sleeve `$34.99`, Tote `$24.99`, Tee `$32.99`) are set for healthy profit margins. Would you like to keep these prices as-is, run a holiday launch discount (like 15% off), or adjust any price?"
- **Recommendation:** "Keep these base prices and offer a 15% Holiday Launch code for email subscribers to grow your list fast."

### Question 4 — Blog & Research Cadence
- **Ask:** "How often would you like me to research the newest worldwide mushroom studies and draft a new blog post for your review?"
- **Recommendation:** "One fresh, deeply cited article every week (sent to you as a quick 30-second preview before publishing) builds steady Google search traffic without overwhelming you."

### Question 5 — Approval Style (Autopilot vs. Quick Check-In)
- **Ask:** "Which things would you like me to handle automatically behind the scenes, and which things do you want a quick 'yes/no' check-in on before they go live?"
- **Recommendation:** "Let me automatically research studies, draft blogs, design seasonal merch drafts, and find partnerships — and always send you a quick 1-tap preview before publishing a new post, changing prices, or spending any money."

### Question 6 — Partnerships & Income Streams
- **Ask:** "Beyond your own merch store, which extra income streams feel aligned with Sweet Psilocybe? For example: affiliate links for trusted functional mushroom brands (Lion's Mane/Reishi coffee), book/documentary affiliate links in your 3D Library, or sponsored educational newsletters?"
- **Recommendation:** "Starting with book links in your 3D Library plus 1–2 vetted functional mushroom partners is effortless, high-trust, and keeps the brand 100% authentic."

Summarize all 6 decisions in 6 short bullet points, save them to `ops/AARON_PREFERENCES.md`, and immediately propose **the single highest-value first action** (e.g., swapping in her social handles + drafting this week's holiday promo post!).
